## 2. The 3-Minute Live Demonstration Script

```text
[0:00 - 0:30] THE HOOK & PROBLEM:
"Every astronaut who walked on the Moon trained in terrestrial analogs on Earth. But Earth is NOT the Moon or Mars.
Current analog tools are either static 40-page PDFs or simplistic maps that claim a desert is '80% like Mars'.
This lack of rigor risks hardware failure and lost missions.
Welcome to TERRALIGN: Where Earth Meets Other Worlds."

[0:30 - 1:15] THE CORE DIFFERENTIATOR (ACTIVITY-SPECIFIC MATCHING):
"We don't score a location in isolation. We score it against a mission.
Let's select 'Artemis Lunar South Pole EVA Sampling'.
Notice how TERRALIGN ranks the Iceland Askja Caldera and Cinder Lake, Arizona at the top,
while explicitly flagging the Analog Reality Gap: 1.0g gravity versus 0.166g lunar gravity,

[1:15 - 2:00] THE HERO 3D EXPERIENCE (LUNAR LIGHTING SIMULATOR):
"Here is where TERRALIGN becomes indispensable. At the lunar South Pole, the Sun never rises more than 3 degrees above the horizon.
Watch our interactive lighting engine in real time.
As we lower the solar elevation to 1.5 degrees, see the razor-sharp pitch-black shadows stretch across the crater rim.
This is exactly the optical challenge Artemis III astronauts will face. No other tool simulates this on terrestrial terrain."

[2:00 - 2:30] MULTI-SITE REHEARSAL PORTFOLIO & DISCOVERY:
"No single location can rehearse an entire mission.
With one click, TERRALIGN's portfolio optimizer generates a 3-site rehearsal itinerary:

covering 96% of mission test gates."

[2:30 - 3:00] THE CLOSE:
"TERRALIGN is not a concept. It is a scientifically grounded, open-data intelligence platform
built on NASA Planetary Data System and USGS elevation models.
TERRALIGN bridges the reality gap between Earth and other worlds. Thank you."
```
