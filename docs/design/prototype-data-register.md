# TERRALIGN Prototype Data Register & Scientific Claim Ledger

> **Document**: `docs/design/prototype-data-register.md`  
> **Phase**: Phase 2 — Prototype Data Integrity & Scientific Label Audit (`PRM-105`)  
> **Standard**: Zero Fabricated Claims · Full Provenance Disclosure · Transparent Epistemic Classification  
> **Classification Taxa**:
> - `[VERIFIED SOURCE DATA]`: Direct in-situ or orbital measurement from primary literature / official government agency datasets.
> - `[DERIVED FROM VERIFIED DATA]`: Mathematical derivation from verified physical constants or published data with documented formulas.
> - `[ILLUSTRATIVE DEMO DATA]`: Mock data or unvalidated design-time values used for interface demonstration.
> - `[UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]`: Theoretical target or research assumption requiring empirical validation in future phases.
> - `[UNCHARACTERIZED]`: Parameter lacking field or laboratory measurements at the specified location.
